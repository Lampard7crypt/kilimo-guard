1. Cash Flow & Profitability: Total verified sales vs operating expenses, profit margin stability, revenue volume.
2. Production & Acreage Risk: Crop diversification (mono-crop vs mixed cropping), acreage scale, yield risk.
3. SACCO & Institutional Standing: SACCO membership status and verification.
4. Operational & Scouting Discipline: Frequency of ledger logging and adherence to farm management.
